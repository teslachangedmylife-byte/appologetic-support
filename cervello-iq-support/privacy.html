Privacy Policy

How CERVELLO IQ handles your information.

Information CERVELLO IQ collects

CERVELLO IQ collects no personal data. The app has no account, no login, no tracking, no analytics, and no ads. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and it does not sell or share any information about you.

Information saved on your device

The app works entirely on your device. Your training history, exercise scores, streaks, achievements, and reminders are stored on your device only. The start of your free trial is stored on your device only. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to any other party.

Microphone and camera

CERVELLO IQ does not use the microphone or the camera, and it does not request access to either.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device to remind you to train. Reminders are created and delivered on your device only. No reminder data leaves your device.

Purchases

Membership is a subscription processed by Apple through the App Store. A 3-day free trial is included; after the trial, membership renews at Weekly $4.99, Monthly $9.99, or Annual $49.99, depending on the plan you choose. You can cancel anytime. We receive no payment details, card numbers, or billing information. Apple handles the purchase, the trial, and the receipt under its own privacy policy.

Links

Articles in the Learn section link to the National Institute on Aging website, and the Clinical Screening Guide links to the websites of the screening-tool publishers. Those websites handle information under their own privacy policies. If you email support, the developer receives the address and information you choose to send and uses it only to respond to you. Contact support to request deletion of your support correspondence.

Health information

CERVELLO IQ is a brain-training game app for cognitive wellness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease or condition. The app's scores measure exercise performance only. If you have a health concern, please speak to a healthcare professional.

Retention and deletion

CERVELLO IQ keeps no information about you on any server, so there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. The training history saved on your device is removed when you delete the app from your device.
