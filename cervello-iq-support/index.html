App Support

Help with CERVELLO IQ on iPhone.

What CERVELLO IQ is

CERVELLO IQ is a brain-training app. It has 27 games that train memory, focus, attention, and mental clarity, a daily workout, a progress chart, a Learn library with short articles about brain health, and achievements to unlock as you train. Everything works on your device — no account and no login.

How to use it

Open the app and tap Start on your daily workout, or pick any game from the Train tab. Tap your answer and the app moves on to the next question by itself. Your scores and streaks appear on the Home and Progress tabs. Scores measure exercise performance only — they are not a medical test or diagnosis.

Free trial and membership

Your first 3 days are free. After the trial, membership renews automatically at the price of the plan you chose: Weekly $4.99, Monthly $9.99, or Annual $49.99. Membership unlocks the full app: all brain-training games, progress tracking, and the learning library. You can cancel anytime and keep training until the end of the current period.

Restore Purchase

If you already have a membership and it is not showing on a new device or after reinstalling, open the Membership page and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase. Restoring does not charge you again.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap CERVELLO IQ, then tap Cancel Subscription. You can also manage it from the Membership page in the app by tapping Manage Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your membership stays active until the end of the current period.

Daily reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app sends a local notification on your device to remind you to train. You can change or turn off reminders in the app's settings at any time.

Contact CERVELLO IQ

Email castaldostudio@mac.com for help. Include your app version and iPhone model if relevant. See our Privacy Policy for how your information is handled.
