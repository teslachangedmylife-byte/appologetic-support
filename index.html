App Support

Help with Appologetic on iPhone and iPad.

Contact Appologetic

Email teslachangedmylife@gmail.com for help, accessibility feedback, or a correction to a study. Include your app version and device model if relevant. Keep private reflections and other personal details out of bug reports.

Change your study perspective

Open Profile and choose Change study perspective to switch between the Christian, Muslim, and shared views.

Manage saved data

My Journey lets you delete reflections and bookmarks. Open Profile and choose Reset local data to clear your journal, bookmarks, perspective preference, and introduction progress.

Search and summaries

Search matches questions to the included studies. When Apple Intelligence is available, you can request an on-device summary after finding a study. The passages, commentary, and journal are available without an Apple Intelligence device.
