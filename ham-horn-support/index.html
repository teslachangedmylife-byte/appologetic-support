Help with HAM HORN AI on iPhone.

What HAM HORN AI is

HAM HORN AI is The Pocket Air Horn. It has four loud horn sounds: GOAT, CLASSIC, MIX, and LOSER. Tap AI PICK and the app picks the perfect horn for the moment, on your device. You get 5 free blasts to try them all, then a one-time $1.99 purchase unlocks unlimited blasts, forever.

How to use it

Tap a horn button — GOAT, CLASSIC, MIX, or LOSER — and the sound plays all the way to the end. One tap uses one free blast. Tap AI PICK to let the app choose a horn for you; that also uses one free blast. Lifting your finger does not stop the sound.

Free blasts and the paywall

You start with 5 free blasts, shared across all four sounds and AI PICK. When your free blasts are used up, the paywall appears. Tap UNLOCK UNLIMITED to buy unlimited blasts for $1.99, once. Tap the X, tap outside the paywall, or swipe it down to close it without buying — you will not get extra free blasts by closing it.

Restore Purchase

If you already bought Unlimited Blasts and it is not showing on a new device or after reinstalling, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used for the purchase. Restoring does not charge you again.

Sound is not playing

Check that your iPhone is not on silent (the Ring/Silent switch) and turn the volume up. If the sound still does not play, close the app fully and reopen it, or restart your iPhone. HAM HORN AI does not use the microphone or the camera.
