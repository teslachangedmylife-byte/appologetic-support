Privacy Policy

How HAM HORN AI handles your information.

Information HAM HORN AI collects

HAM HORN AI collects no personal data. The app has no account, no login, no tracking, no analytics, and no ads. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers, and it does not sell or share any information about you.

Information saved on your device

The app works entirely on your device. The four horn sounds (GOAT, CLASSIC, MIX, and LOSER) are audio files stored in the app. Your remaining free blasts are stored on your device only. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to any other party.

AI PICK

The AI PICK feature runs entirely on your device. It picks one of the four horn sounds for you, on-device only. It does not send any information anywhere, and it does not use a server, a cloud service, or the internet.

Microphone and camera

HAM HORN AI does not use the microphone or the camera, and it does not request access to either.

Purchases

The $1.99 Unlimited Blasts purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details, card numbers, or billing information. Apple handles the purchase and the receipt under its own privacy policy.

Links and support

Links in the app or on this site may open a website. That website handles information under its own privacy policy. If you email support, the developer receives the address and information you choose to send and uses it only to respond to you. Contact support to request deletion of your support correspondence.

Retention and deletion

HAM HORN AI keeps no information about you on any server, so there is nothing to retain or delete on our side. The free-blast count saved on your device is removed when you delete the app from your device.

Privacy questions
