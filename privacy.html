Privacy Policy

How Appologetic handles your information.

Information Appologetic collects

Appologetic does not collect personal data from the app. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Appologetic does not sell or share your journal, bookmarks, questions, or study perspective.

Information saved on your device

Your study perspective and introduction progress are saved in app settings. Appologetic saves the bookmarks and reflections you choose to keep in a local database protected by iOS or iPadOS. The database is excluded from device backups. Your chosen perspective only changes how the app presents the studies.

Search and optional summaries

Questions are matched against the study library on your device. Appologetic does not save questions or search results. If you request a summary on a supported device, the Apple Intelligence model processes the app’s editorial study notes on your device. The model does not receive your typed question, saved journal, or selected perspective.

Links and support

Source and support links may open a website. That website handles information under its own privacy policy. If you email support, the publisher receives the address and information you choose to send and uses it to respond. Contact support to request deletion of your support correspondence.

Retention and deletion

Saved information stays in the app until you remove it. In My Journey, you can delete an individual reflection or clear reflections and bookmarks together. In Profile, select Reset local data to clear your journal, bookmarks, study perspective, and introduction progress. Deleting the app from your device also removes its local data.
